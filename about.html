About Us

Welcome to DocuCraft AI, your all-in-one browser-based productivity suite designed to make document management and daily utility tasks effortless, fast, and secure.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide powerful digital tools that everyone can use for free, without worrying about privacy. We believe that converting documents, editing PDFs, and handling daily calculations should be simple, instantaneous, and accessible directly within your web browser.

Privacy First: Client-Side Processing

At DocuCraft AI, your privacy is our top priority. Unlike traditional online tools that require you to upload your sensitive documents to a server, most of our tools process your files directly inside your browser using advanced client-side JavaScript. This means your confidential files, contracts, and personal photos never leave your device, ensuring maximum security and peace of mind.

Built for Everyone

Whether you need to merge PDFs, resize images for government job applications, calculate loan EMIs, or generate quick notes, DocuCraft AI is built to handle your everyday tasks efficiently.
