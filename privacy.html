Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data Privacy & Processing

At DocuCraft AI, your privacy is our top priority. Most of our PDF tools, image compressors, and generators process your files directly inside your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your confidential files and photos do not get uploaded or saved to our servers unless specifically required by an external service.

2. Information Collection

We do not store, view, or distribute your documents. Standard website analytics (like Google Analytics) may collect anonymous usage data such as browser type and visit duration to help us improve site performance.

3. External Services

When you use optional AI assistant features or online OCR services, the required text or queries are transmitted securely via encrypted connections (HTTPS) only to perform the requested function.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect non-personal information (such as your browser type, time and date, and interaction patterns) to display relevant advertisements about goods and services that may interest you.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to DocuCraft AI and other websites across the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or through AboutAds.info.

5. Log Files

DocuCraft AI follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us directly at: support@docucraftai.website.
