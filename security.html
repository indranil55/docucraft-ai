Security Overview

Last updated: September 2026

Client-Side Security

DocuCraft AI is architected with modern web technologies that prioritize client-side execution. When you compress an image, merge PDFs, or generate signatures, the operations are executed within your local device memory.

HTTPS & Transport Encryption

All interactions with our domain are enforced over TLS/HTTPS to protect your session and safeguard data in transit against eavesdropping or interception.

Zero Document Retention

Because files are processed on the client side, we do not maintain temporary or permanent cloud storage for your documents, ensuring complete data ownership remains with you.
